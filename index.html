Privacy Policy for ZipZep

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZipZep ("the app", "we", "us"), a puzzle game for Android (package name com.kmvnetwork.zipzep) published by Sonu Purbiya, handles information when you use the app. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. What we do not collect

ZipZep does not ask you to create an account or log in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or precise location. The app does not request any of these permissions.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (such as your current level) and settings (such as sound on/off) locally on your device only. This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising and third-party services

ZipZep is free and shows ads through Unity Ads, a service provided by Unity Technologies. To show ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud, Unity Ads may collect and share the following information from your device:

  • Device and other identifiers, including your Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version)
  • Interactions with ads, such as ad views and taps
  • App diagnostics and usage information related to the ad experience

This data is collected and processed by Unity under its own privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. We do not receive your personal information from Unity and we do not sell your personal information.

The app needs an internet connection only to load ads. The game itself can be played without sending your gameplay data to us.

4. Your choices about ads

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your Android device settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads; the exact path depends on your device).
  • Rewarded ads in the game are optional. You choose whether to watch them.
  • You can stop all data collection by the ad service by uninstalling the app.

5. Children's privacy

ZipZep is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will help to have it removed.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not store your personal data on our servers. Data saved on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app. For data held by Unity, you can make access or deletion requests through Unity's privacy policy page linked above. You may also contact us and we will assist you.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the app and its content. Data sent to the advertising service is transmitted over secure connections. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of it. To exercise these rights, contact us or Unity using the details in this policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
